<i>Glass Vultures</i> is an anthology on the theme of death. The purpose was to create short films, learning from the inevitable mistakes we would make in the process.  This was also an opportunity to work with local talent.

We bought the equipment, and planned and filmed the first episode, which is currently in post-production.

We overcame the hurdles of acquiring permission for locations in which to shoot, making storyboards, and the constraints of time. We adapted to problems as they occurred.

Episode one is in post-production. Episodes two and three are currently in pre-production.

## Equipment
- BlackMagic Pocket Cinema Camera 6k
- Weebill 2 Gimbal
- Kenro Twin Tube Tripod
- Zoom H1n Recorder + Rode SmartPlusMic Lavalier
- DaVinci Resolve
