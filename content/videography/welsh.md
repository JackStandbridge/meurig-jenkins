I am a film maker from Aberystwyth, now living in Anglesey, North Wales.

My films are inspired by situations and personalities both local and distant. I always seek to explore the oddities of everyday life.

I practically grew up in the theatre. I performed in plays (<i>The Goalkeeper’s Revenge; The Roses of Eyam; Twelfth Night</i>), musicals and operettas (<i>Bugsy Malone; The Sorcerer’s Apprentice; Ruddigore</i>). I took part in the chorus of pantomimes (<i>Aladdin; Beauty and the Beast</i>) and took leading roles in summer seasons at Aberystwyth Arts Centre (<i>The King and I; The Sound of Music; Peter Pan</i>).

Now I’m pursuing a career in the film industry as an actor, writer and director. I’m seeking to refine my style, and explore as many opportunities as possible.

Please <a href="mailto:meurigjenkinscyd@gmail.com">get in touch</a> if you are interested in collaborating, or can offer me any opportunities to take part in your project.
